##A laravel app named ContentHub - A publishing and engagemetn platform

#Overview
simplified content publishing and engagement platform. user should be able to create and share posts, interact through comments and reveive timely notification. The system must ensure scalability, reliability and maintainability while delivering smooth user experience.


#Objectives
- register, authenticae, manage profile
- enable publishing, editing, delete contents by authenticated users
- allow readers to view and engage published contents
- provide mechanism for iteraction and feedback through comments
- ensure users are notified of relevant events and updates
- demonstrate performance consideration


#Deliverables
- a working applicaiton that fullfils the objectives and scope
- documentation describing:
 -- setup and installaiton
 -- assumptions during development
 -- known limitations and potential future improvements.
